Insights

Capture: "I simulated a 24-hour capture of about 1.28 million packets and show the live stream with suspicious packets flagged."

Analyze: "Each chart answers one question. For example, every SYN should get a SYN-ACK. 7,300 that didn't means a port scan."

Detect: "Three critical signals all point to one host, 10.0.4.27: a 03:00 bandwidth spike, DNS tunneling and C2 beaconing."

Act: "Grouping alerts by host cuts 37 alerts to 13 priorities. Blocking two domains costs almost nothing. MFA stops the brute-force risk."
